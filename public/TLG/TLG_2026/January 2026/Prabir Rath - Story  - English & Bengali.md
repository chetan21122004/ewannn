# The Ant 

Prabir kumar Rath 

A crow was sitting, in the branch of an olive tree. It was near a cascade, far away from the mountain foot of main Mumbai city and the districts. Time was the then when Pandavas and Kauravas and Lord Krishna were illuminating the earth. The crow was on its fast, since in the morning that day. The olive tree on which the crow sat, the great pandava Bhim used to come to endow food to ants that lived under the tree in a hole. Bhim gave them immense food, not the food grains, the crow thus got its good meal, and it thought there was no harm in taking the morsel, from such huge pile or immense endowment, but what happened, for the last two days the great Pandav was not coming to give food to the ants. The crow though knew the reason that, when the day before yesterday Bhim came under the tree, Lord Krishna all on a sudden, happened to be there, and the crow heard him saying not to misuse anything, in the name of offering something, and thus no Dharma or Karma actually works, and as a result, no outcome of blessing is got. Bhim was silent, but smile of Lord Krishna hurt the crow terribly. No question was there relating to food offered by Bhim to the ants, as being royal. The crow suddenly saw millions and millions of ants were coming out from under the earth and were moving forward towards the cascade. The crow got perplexed, thinking, what fate had designed for the ants as well as, the crow. 

Being interested the crow, came down to the lower branches, what it saw, did not only, teach him a lesson, but also make it a transformed creature. The crow heard, from among millions of ant only one the smallest one, led such great army, to proceed further towards Dwarka to ask the lord of lords Krishna a difficult question, "Why he is so jealous of someone's gain without achievement?" 

The crow tried to prevent them in vain, flew away to achieve its daily need, but the ants are still moving forward in search of answer to find the Lord.
