# **<mark>When sadness gave me joy</mark>** 

<mark>My numb body lies comatose on a cold, crumpled bed And it's Shiv Kumar Batalvi's soft, sad, melodic voice Drifting in from the unplugged speakers in the attic Of a snowed in duplex, six thousand fve hundred fourteen kilometers from home, That frees the tears from my glassy eyes, Turns the pinched straight line into a bittersweet smile And starts melting the black ice that has frozen my heart Into what seemed to be an interminable emotional hibernation.</mark> 

<mark>Each word of</mark> _<mark>Ki puchde ho haal fakiran da</mark>_ <mark>Is accompanied by the trickle of a tear down my pale cheeks And as the warm droplets seep into my barren heart, Sadness blooms inside, And my face lights up with a thousand smiles.</mark>
