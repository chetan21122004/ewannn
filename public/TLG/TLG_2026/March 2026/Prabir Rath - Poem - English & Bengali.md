<mark>When.. In Rome</mark> 

Prabir Kumar Rath 

_________________________ Once an omniscient, long long ago, went to Rome Spending only a few cents. 

He was called, by the Roman Emperor, of the time When, reached there, 

No Bugles, no merry-notes Not even a single, Welcome was sent. Nor did the Omniscient,  find A Place, to stay and  rest. 

The whole Kingdom was decked with flower, scent and saints On mammoth hoardings. Everywhere 

They were inscribed with Raman Dialect, The Omniscient could not catch, being discontented, He said, 

Why have I been insulted by your race. 

The emperor not surprised replied "Bun venit" 

The man was Omniscient There was a language barrier. 

His omniscience was vanished 

An Idiom was born, "When in Rome do as Romans do"
